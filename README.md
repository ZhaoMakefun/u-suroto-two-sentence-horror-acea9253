This entry is a horror story in exactly two sentences: "My doorbell camera sends me a photo every time someone rings, and tonight it sent one of me standing on my own porch. I haven't left the house all day, and the photo was taken from inside." The second sentence turns the first — a familiar smart-home scene becomes a break-in that already happened — and the horror is left implied, never explained.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` by default), exactly 188 characters in two sentences, no trailing newline.
